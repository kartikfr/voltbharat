# VOLT Bharat - Brand Guide & Design Language

This document serves as the single source of truth for the brand narrative, core mission, design tokens, typography scales, content specifications, and website copywriting guidelines for **VOLT Bharat** (Voice Of Latest Trends in Bharat).

---

## 1. Brand Narrative & Name Story

### The Core Story
India's renewable energy transition is entering the most critical decade in its history. Policies are changing, subsidies are opening new opportunities, green manufacturing is scaling, and capital markets are merging into one connected clean-energy economy. 

However, information is scattered. Founders miss policy updates, companies miss subsidy windows, and investors miss early trends. 

**VOLT Bharat exists to turn clean-energy noise into trusted signals.**

### The Meaning Behind the Name
The name **VOLT Bharat** operates on two distinct layers:
1. **Volt as Energy**: Instantly connects the brand with electricity, grid power, clean energy, solar, storage, and the future of utility infrastructure.
2. **VOLT as Voice Of Latest Trends**: Establishes the brand’s editorial, intelligence-led, and analytical authority. We don't just report news; we decode it.
3. **Bharat**: Establishes ownership, geography, and deep local relevance. This is built specifically for India’s policymakers, founders, investors, and industrial clean-tech ecosystem.

## 4. Exact Colour System & Tokens

The website design must strictly adhere to the locked brand color system to ensure authority and premium editorial aesthetics.

### Primary Palette
| Color Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Core Brand Color | **Intelligence Blue** | `#0D1B3D` | Main headings, footer backgrounds, dark sections, authority & trust. |
| Positive Indicator | **Renewable Green** | `#1FA463` | "Bharat" in logo, CTAs, hover states, highlights, clean energy growth. |
| Telemetry Signal | **Signal Yellow** | `#FFC107` | Signal dots, alert tags, data dividers, active states. **Use sparingly like a signal.** |

### Supporting Palette
| Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Steel Blue Accent** | `#4B5B78` | Secondary body text, borders on dark panels, muted labels. |
| **Light Grey** | `#F2F4F7` | Card backgrounds, panel borders, neutral dividers. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Main page background, light cards, positive text. |
| **Soft Ivory** | `#F8FAF6` | Editorial warmth, premium calm sections, hero backing. |
| **Data Mist Blue** | `#EAF4FF` | Subtle data grids, chart backdrops, hover backgrounds. |

### Color Usage Ratios (Light Theme)
To maintain a high-end, premium look without appearing too playful, color weights should align with the following ratio:
- **White / Soft Ivory**: `55%`
- **Intelligence Blue**: `22%`
- **Renewable Green**: `12%`
- **Light Grey / Data Mist Blue**: `8%`
- **Signal Yellow**: `3%` (Must only be used as accent tags/signal indicators).

### Gradient Specifications
- **Primary Light Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #F8FAF6 50%, #EAF4FF 100%)` (Hero section, content backing)
- **Premium Dark Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #0D1B3D 0%, #07142F 50%, #031024 100%)` (Footer, dark subscription block)
- **Signal Accent Gradient**: `linear-gradient(90deg, #1FA463 0%, #FFC107 100%)` (Thin lines, progress trackers, animated paths)

## 5. Typography System

Typography represents the voice of the platform. Using Google Fonts, we declare:

- **Headings Font**: **Sora** (Sora Bold / Sora SemiBold)
  - *Feel*: Modern, intelligent, tech-forward, and strong.
- **Body Text Font**: **Inter** (Inter Regular / Inter Medium)
  - *Feel*: Highly readable, clean, and professional.
- **Data & Metric Labels Font**: **Space Grotesk** (Space Grotesk Medium / Space Grotesk Bold)
  - *Feel*: Technical, structured, and ideal for timelines, counts, and charts.

### 2. Meaning Behind VOLT Bharat (Name Story)
- **Section Eyebrow**: The Meaning Behind VOLT Bharat
- **Headline**: Voice. Energy. Signals. Bharat.
- **Body Copy**:
  VOLT Bharat stands for Voice Of Latest Trends in Bharat. The word VOLT carries the energy of the sector we track: electricity, clean power, solar, wind, storage, grid, and the future of energy.

It represents a belief that Bharat’s renewable-energy sector needs a trusted voice, one that can track the market, decode the policies, simplify subsidies, follow capital-market movements, and explain the clean-tech shifts shaping the future.

## 7. Editorial Rules

- **Zero Clutter**: Avoid overly playful styling, generic icons, or illustrations. Focus on high-contrast, structured data, data gridlines, and clean typography.
- **No Placeholder Content**: All content must represent realistic telemetry, real circular updates, and real clean-energy metrics.
- **Tone**: Professional, analytical, authoritative, and strategic.
